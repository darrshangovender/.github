# Code of conduct

These projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussion on the technical merits, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that do not meet this standard.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **darrshangovender@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
